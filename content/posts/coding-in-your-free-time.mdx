# Coding in Your Free Time

Small, consistent efforts outside work or study lead to massive growth.

## Why It Matters

- Skill improvement
- Strong portfolio
- Better problem-solving

## Start Small

Break projects into manageable tasks and grow gradually.

## Project Ideas

- Portfolio site
- To-do app
- Calculator
- Weather app
- Blog

![Coding in Free Time](/images/coding(end).png)

## Conclusion

Don’t rush. Start small, stay consistent, and let progress compound.
